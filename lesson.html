What you will learn

Build safer habits online

Understanding digital footprints helps people protect identity, privacy, and well-being across apps, schools, and communities.

Footprint

What is a digital footprint?

A digital footprint is the trail left behind by posts, searches, messages, likes, and app activity. It can be public, private, or permanent.

Privacy

Why privacy matters

Oversharing can affect school, jobs, and safety. Privacy settings, location controls, and careful posting protect personal data.

Respect

Responsible social media

Healthy digital habits include fact-checking, respectful communication, and thinking before sharing content with a wide audience.

Secure

Protect your accounts

Use a different, strong password for each account and turn on multi-factor authentication to make unauthorized access harder.

Spot

Recognize scams and misinformation

Pause before clicking urgent links or sharing surprising claims. Check the source, look for supporting evidence, and confirm requests through a trusted channel.

Location

Control what your posts reveal

Review app permissions and avoid sharing live locations, school details, routines, or photos that reveal private information in the background.

Repair

Improve your digital footprint

Review old posts, delete content that no longer represents you, update privacy settings, and ask platforms or people to remove information when appropriate.
