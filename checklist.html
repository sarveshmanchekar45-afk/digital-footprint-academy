Quick checklist

Protect your personal information

Use these checks before sharing content or changing account settings.

Safe sharing habits

  • ✓Pause before posting: would you be comfortable if a teacher, family member, or employer saw it?
  • ✓Turn off location tags and review camera permissions on apps.
  • ✓Keep your address, school, and phone number private.
  • ✓Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

Smart account settings

  • ✓Review privacy settings regularly across every platform.
  • ✓Limit who can comment, tag, or contact you.
  • ✓Be careful with third-party app access and public Wi-Fi.
  • ✓Report or block suspicious or harmful behavior.

Advantages of social media safety

  • ✓Protects your identity, personal information, and online reputation.
  • ✓Helps you connect with friends, communities, and support networks more confidently.
  • ✓Makes it easier to learn, share useful information, and discover opportunities.
  • ✓Reduces the chance of scams, account theft, cyberbullying, and unwanted contact.

Disadvantages when safety is ignored

  • ✓Oversharing can expose your location, routines, or other private details.
  • ✓False information and harmful content can spread quickly.
  • ✓Cyberbullying, pressure to compare yourself, and excessive screen time can affect wellbeing.
  • ✓Weak passwords and suspicious links can lead to hacked accounts, fraud, or identity theft.
