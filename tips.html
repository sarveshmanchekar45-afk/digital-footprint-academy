Safety habits

Daily tips for a safer online life

Think before posting

Ask if the content could affect your future opportunities or personal safety.

Verify before trusting

Check sources and avoid sharing rumors, scams, or personal information in public chats.

Set boundaries

Manage friends, followers, and who can view your stories, photos, and contact info.

Talk to a trusted adult

If something online feels unsafe or uncomfortable, reach out for support immediately.
